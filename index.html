About the Manor

The whole house, to yourselves

Shani Manor is let as a single, entire villa — never split between strangers, never shared with another party. It suits families gathering for a milestone, friends travelling together, or anyone who simply prefers a house that feels like their own for the length of a stay.

Four air-conditioned rooms sit comfortably around the house, with space for up to nine guests. A swimming pool, free Wi-Fi throughout, and a peaceful atmosphere set the pace: quiet mornings by the water, long unhurried meals, a house that adjusts to your rhythm rather than the other way around.

Hikkaduwa Beach is less than 10 minutes away, so the coast is always within easy reach — without ever feeling like you're staying in the middle of it.

A house taken whole, not a room taken in turn. The idea behind Shani Manor
