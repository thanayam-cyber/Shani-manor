History of Shani Manor

Built in 1927.

Shani Manor carries the physical character of an earlier Sri Lanka. The original house was constructed using clay, metal, cabook (laterite stone) and lime.

Its approximately 18-inch-thick walls and traditional materials give the property a sense of permanence that is difficult to reproduce today.

The roof tiles were imported from the United Kingdom, adding another chapter to the story of this remarkable coastal home.

Frequently asked

Good to know.

Can I book an individual room?

Yes. Individual rooms are available at USD 75 per room per night with breakfast included, for up to 2 adults.

Can I book the entire villa?

Yes. The entire four-bedroom villa is available at USD 260 per night for up to 9 guests.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is included with individual room bookings under the BB plan. For entire-villa bookings, food is charged at cost with a 20% food preparation/service charge. Tea and coffee are included.

How far is Hikkaduwa Beach?

Shani Manor is located in Hikkaduwa, with Hikkaduwa Beach less than 10 minutes away.

Do guests have access to the pool and Wi-Fi?

Yes. Guests have access to the swimming pool and complimentary Wi-Fi.
